An interactive learning app that transforms English 111 curriculum exercises into questions and answers. It helps trainees practise, review lessons, receive immediate feedback, and prepare for final assessments through comprehensive course revision, enhancing their understanding and practical use of English and promote independent learning through interactive practice and continuous feedback.

English language 1 – Eng.111 : interactive activities app

HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE
1. Upload this whole folder to any website host that uses HTTPS (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or your college server).
2. Send students the link. They open it and tap "Install app" (Android/Chrome) or Share > Add to Home Screen (iPhone).
3. After the first visit it works without internet.

Students' answers are saved on their own phone only. Results are copied or shared by the student from the Results tab.

SEND ONE TUTORIAL ALONE
Add ?t=NUMBER to the link, for example  https://your-site/?t=3  opens Tutorial 3 only (no other tabs). The app also has a "Copy link of this tutorial" button under every tutorial, and a list of all links in the Results tab.
Each tutorial is marked out of 20. To change this, edit  const MAX = 20  at the top of app.js.
